Course Title AEE 321 – MICROPROCESSOR SYSTEMS 
Course Description 
The course includes history and evolution, principles, and applications of microprocessors. The 
focus is on the basic understanding of the architectural design, functional parts, operations, 
function, and programming. It also covers the study of various types of microprocessors and the 
fundamental concepts of microcontrollers.
